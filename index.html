Mission Statement

Cooking for Beginners is meant to serve as an easily approachable learning resources for anyone new to cooking to improve their skills, or for experienced chefs to brush up on the basics.

Content Pages

This website organizes its cooking information content into different pages, accessible via the top menu bar. To summarize them briefly:

Food Safety

  • Contains food safety practices best followed by anyone preparing food
  • While some food safety practices are considered universal rules, professional kitchens must follow even more exacting safety and hygiene regulations for legal purposes. This page does not provide those.

Recipes

  • A collection of recipes selected for practicing your cooking skills
  • Our recipe pages additionally contain information such as which kitchen equipment will be needed, or which food safety rules may come into play

Cooking Equipment

  • Contains instructions on how to use many common cooking utensils

FAQ

  • Answers several cooking questions commonly asked by novices
